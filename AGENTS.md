# Owner publication constraint

All project assets must remain private while work is in progress, including
GitHub repositories, Kaggle datasets, models, notebooks, reports and source.
The owner must complete a final review and explicitly approve publication
before any asset is made public. Broad authorization to proceed, improve a
score, meet competition requirements, or create ownership copies does not
authorize public visibility. Prepare the entire private package before asking
for that final approval. Do not publish automatically at a deadline.

Specific owner exception on 2026-11-14: keep the already-public duplicate
`shepscientific/filament-segmentation-2026-final-assets` (dataset ID 13351744)
in place for now. The owner declined its permanent deletion. Do not delete,
update or expand that public copy without further instruction. This exception
does not authorize any other public asset or repository.

Keep the official competition's final eligibility requirements on the checklist,
but resolve them through the owner's final review. Preserve source and weights
when restricting access or removing redundant public copies.
